**2. `register`'s catch block now leaks config-validation text.** `register/route.ts:90` returns `Registration failed: ${error.message}`. Post-Phase-1/2, a misconfigured deploy makes that message the Zod env-validation string (e.g. "JWT_SECRET must be at least 32 characters") rather than a driver error. No secret *values* leak, and **Phase 9 explicitly makes this generic** — so this is scoped-out, just flagging that the surface shifted slightly rather than shrank. `login`/`refresh` already return generic text.
