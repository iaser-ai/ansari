## Why — and this is NOT "the lane is flaky"

The architect tested the claude lane independently from the architect side during
the outage: a trivial prompt completed in **3.9s**, against lane-wide stats of 86%
success / 151s average. The CLI and the model are both healthy. Calling this
"flaky" would be false, and worse, it would stop anyone from investigating.

**Hypothesis (architect's, and it fits every data point): phase_2 is the phase
that introduced a persistent, never-terminating task.** `turbo run dev` does not
exit — by design. An agentic reviewer with shell access that decides to *exercise*
the dev task hangs forever.

The evidence matches precisely:

- **Zero bytes written**, not partial output — consistent with a held pipe, not a
  model thinking slowly.
- **SIGTERM ignored, SIGKILL required** — the signature of a child process group
  holding the pipe open.
- **Lane-by-lane pattern**: claude (Agent SDK, tool use) wedged 3×; gemini
  (agentic, `--sandbox`) timed out once and needed a relaunch; codex completed
  normally. **The two file-agentic lanes are exactly the two that hung** — on
  precisely the phase that added a task which never returns.

Alternatives ruled out by the architect: `.turbo` directories are trivial (124K
max) and gitignored; the phase_2 diff is small (211 insertions across 5 files);
and while the branch total is 232 files, phase_1 reviewed cleanly three times
against that same large diff.
