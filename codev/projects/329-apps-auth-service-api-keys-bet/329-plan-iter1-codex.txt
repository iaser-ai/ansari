KEY_ISSUES:

- Mounting before CORS is insufficient unless the exchange router terminates `OPTIONS`; otherwise it falls through to `corsFor`. Specify an explicit no-CORS `OPTIONS` handler.
- Specify route-local JSON error middleware mapping malformed and oversized bodies to the exact 400 contract without Express error pages or body leakage.

- Operator-script Sentry events may be lost on process exit. Require Sentry initialization and awaited `flush`/`close` after create, rotate, and revoke.
- Add explicit tests for expired bans, delegation inputs (admin/email IDs and emails, Google account IDs), per-user rate-limit key contents, and confirmation that every key refusal creates no identity/session rows.
