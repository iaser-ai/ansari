VERDICT: REQUEST_CHANGES
SUMMARY: Core implementation is strong, but signing after commit breaks the specification’s locking invariant.
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
---
KEY_ISSUES:
- `issueWhatsappToken` commits before `signJWT`. A concurrent ban, deletion, or admin promotion can commit between eligibility/session checks and signing, allowing issuance after promotion or producing a JWT for a deleted session. Signing must remain protected by the decision lock or the specification must be formally revised.
- The claim-equivalence test does not directly verify `definePayload` output or JWT `kid`, despite both being explicit phase deliverables.
