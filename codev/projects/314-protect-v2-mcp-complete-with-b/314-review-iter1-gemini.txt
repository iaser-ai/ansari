PR_SUMMARY: |
  ## Summary
  Protects `POST` and `GET /api/v2/mcp-complete` on `apps/api` with `Authorization: Bearer <ansari-mcp key>`, verified on every request at a new public `apps/auth` route (`POST /api/service/verify-key`). Enforces fail-closed semantics (401 on missing/refused keys, 503 on `apps/auth` outage with nothing persisted and no Gemini call), binds thread attribution to the key's `client_id`, and provides a `MCP_COMPLETE_KEY_MODE=log-only` rollout mode (defaulting to `enforce` when unset).

  ## Key Changes
  - **packages/types & packages/auth:** Added `MCP_CLIENT_ID_PATTERN` / `isMcpClientId`, `MCP_KEY_BOUNDS`, `MCP_KEY_INITIAL` (120/60s, 5,000/day), MCP verify alert thresholds, and `clientIdOf` metadata parsing.
  - **apps/auth:** Added `POST /api/service/verify-key` (closed `{ mcp: ['complete'] }` permission set, owner and `ansari-mcp*` client-id binding, `auth.apikey` outage relation check, byte-identical `{"valid":false}` refusals, no CORS, no per-IP limit), generalized key alerts (`createMcpVerifyAlerts` including `client-id-invalid`), required `--client-id` for `ansari-mcp` in `service-keys.ts`, and added a plugin-upgrade guard in `blocked-paths.test.ts`.
  - **apps/api:** Added `verifyServiceKey` in `lib/auth-service/client.ts` (3 s timeout, strict response schema), `MCP_COMPLETE_KEY_MODE` config validation (`enforce` default), and `authorizeMcpRequest` in `v2/mcp-complete/route.ts` with key-derived client attribution and mismatch warnings.
  - **Docs & Config:** Updated `docs/cutover/service-keys-runbook.md`, `docs/self-hosting.md`, `RELEASE.md`, `apps/api` and `apps/mcp` READMEs/env examples, architecture/lessons docs, and `turbo.json` `globalEnv`.

  ## Test Plan

  - `apps/api` pglite and unit suites (`mcp-complete-key.test.ts`, `verify-service-key.test.ts`, `config.test.ts`, `boot-check.test.ts`, plus existing route suites with `helpers/mcp-key.ts`) covering `enforce` and `log-only` modes, 401/503 zero-persistence guarantees, forged `X-Ansari-Client` handling, and key leak scans.
