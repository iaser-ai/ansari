- `docs/cutover/service-keys-runbook.md:378` says a key configured without `Bearer ` can trigger apps/auth’s `unauthorized-volume` alert. It cannot: apps/api classifies this as `missing` and never calls the verify route. Remove that cause or direct operators to the apps/api `missing` verdict instead.
