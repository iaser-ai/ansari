### 1. Spec Adherence
- **Citability representation**: `ToolCallRecord` in [`apps/api/db/schema/messages.ts`](file:///Users/amrmohamed/repos/iaser/ansari/.builders/spir-168/apps/api/db/schema/messages.ts#L53-L61) defines `citations?: Array<{ enabled: boolean }>` as an optional sibling to `content`. `content` remains strictly the unmodified Gemini `functionResponse` payload.
- **Single computation point**: [`apps/api/lib/tools/types.ts`](file:///Users/amrmohamed/repos/iaser/ansari/.builders/spir-168/apps/api/lib/tools/types.ts#L68-L78) introduces and exports `citabilityOf(result: ToolResult)`, which extracts `doc.citations?.enabled === true` for each document in order, failing closed if undefined or false.
- **Both record generation sites updated**:
  - `buildToolResultRecord` in [`apps/api/lib/facilitator/agent.ts`](file:///Users/amrmohamed/repos/iaser/ansari/.builders/spir-168/apps/api/lib/facilitator/agent.ts#L298) populates `citations: citabilityOf(result)`.
  - The inline `budget_skipped` record in [`apps/api/lib/facilitator/agent.ts`](file:///Users/amrmohamed/repos/iaser/ansari/.builders/spir-168/apps/api/lib/facilitator/agent.ts#L941) populates `citations: citabilityOf(skipped)` (evaluating to `[]`, aligned with `content.results: []`).
- **Gemini payload frozen**: `formatToolResultForGemini` was not modified, preserving byte-identity of the functionResponse parts.
- **No database migration**: Citability is encapsulated within the existing JSONB structure of `tool_calls`.

---

### 2. Code Quality & Invariant Safety
- **Array alignment by construction**: Because `formatToolResultForGemini` maps 1:1 over `result.documents` and `citabilityOf` also maps 1:1 over `result.documents`, `content.results.length === citations.length` is guaranteed at write time.
- **Exported block type**: `DocumentContentBlock` is exported from [`apps/api/db/schema/messages.ts`](file:///Users/amrmohamed/repos/iaser/ansari/.builders/spir-168/apps/api/db/schema/messages.ts#L33) ready for consumption by Phase 2 derivation.
- **Clean separation**: `citations` is kept out of `content` and `rawPayload`, ensuring zero serialization leakage to Gemini history replay or existing API endpoints.
