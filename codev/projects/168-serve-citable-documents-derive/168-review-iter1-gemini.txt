### 4. Read Cost & Escalation Handling

- **Storage**: Staging assistant row `pg_column_size(tool_calls)` median is 5.6 KB (p95 14.1 KB, $n=80$), well within the 14 KB median bound.
- **Latency Bound**: Benchmark on a 50-message (25 assistant) thread indicates `/documents` median latency of ~4.9–5.0 ms vs thread GET of ~0.9–1.0 ms (~5× ratio, exceeding the 2× threshold).
  - The root cause is inherent: transferring and parsing 394 KB of document text vs 53 KB of message content.
  - The builder followed spec instructions by escalating the bound violation to the architect via `afx send architect` and marking the PR clearly as merge-blocked pending the owner's decision.

PR_SUMMARY: |
  ## Summary
  Serves source documents (Qur'an, hadith, tafsir, mawsuah) behind assistant answers by deriving them on-demand from existing `messages.tool_calls` JSONB records. Thread GET and share GET remain untouched and byte-identical to pre-change baselines, while sources are served through dedicated `/documents` endpoints joined by `message_index`.

  ## Key Changes
  - **Citability Tracking**: Added `citations: Array<{ enabled: boolean }>` sibling field to stored `tool_result` records via `citabilityOf()`, allowing fail-closed distinction between true hits and system notices (e.g. zero-result searches) without altering Gemini payloads or database schemas.
  - **Isolated Derivation**: Created `lib/db/citable-documents.ts` as the sole serving reader of `tool_calls`. Performs runtime validation, deduplication on `(title, context, data)`, and returns only derived document blocks.
  - **Dedicated Serving Endpoints**: Added authenticated, owner-scoped `GET /api/v2/threads/[id]/documents` and public `GET /api/v2/share/[id]/documents` (reading immutable snapshot data copied at creation time).
  - **Architecture & Invariant Updates**: Amended `arch-critical.md`, `arch.md`, and schema/helper comments to accurately document the new serving reader rule.
  - **Benchmark Suite**: Added `apps/api/scripts/bench-documents.bench.ts` to measure read and latency overhead against staging distributions.
