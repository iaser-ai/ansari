## Hand-off deadlock — checked by reading, it can't stall

This is the one thing that could ship a hung UI, so I traced it rather than trusting the review's claim:

- `advanceReveal` is monotonic for `dt > 0` and the rate floor is unconditional.
- `revealSlice(target, position)` when `position >= target.length` takes `end = target.length`, and the surrogate guard is gated on `end < target.length` — so it *cannot* pull back off the final character. `shown.current === target` is reachable, hence `revealedText === streamingText` is reachable.
- The effect re-runs on every `target`/`settled` change and reschedules, and `cancelAnimationFrame` only ever cancels the id that `tick` most recently assigned to the same local `frame` binding. No stale closure survives a `target` change.
- The rAF re-registration on rapid deltas doesn't starve: registration happens in effects (not inside a rAF callback), so a cancel+re-add still lands in the upcoming frame.

The residual case the review doesn't name: **a backgrounded browser tab pauses rAF**, so the hand-off is deferred while backgrounded. On return, one tick with a huge `dt` snaps to full and the swap fires. Invisible to the user, but worth a line in "Things to Look At".
